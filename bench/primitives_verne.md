session cap 30s per op. Mean is the average over random inputs,
with pilot-bench's own 95% confidence interval on it (`±95% CI`, as a
percent of the mean — its subsession CI, which accounts for
autocorrelation). The ns/op order statistics are the extrema of each
variable-time primitive, from the readings pilot-bench gathered
(`max/min` = 1.0 means data-independent). A `~` on the mean flags a
heavy-tailed op that hit the session cap with its CI still above 10%
(its extrema are the meaningful result); tail resolution tracks the
sample size.
